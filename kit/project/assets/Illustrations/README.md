# Illustrations

Flat scenes in the kit's palette: `accent`, `sticky-yellow`, `sticky-blue`, `ink` outlines on warm paper. They stand in for photos in Polaroid, HotelCard, ExperienceCard and ProductCard until real content arrives, and are carried in the bundle as `Gratifi.ART` (flight, lisbon, pool, room, food, car, cinema, jacket, torn).

- Use one scene per card. Never as a background behind text.
- Real photography from content partners replaces them; keep the same crops (4:3 for stays, 5:3 for experiences).
