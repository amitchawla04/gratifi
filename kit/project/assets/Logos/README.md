# Logos

- `wordmark-dark.svg`: the Gratifi wordmark in `ink` (#141416). Use on `ground`, `screen` or `card`; on dark grounds set the name in type instead until a light version is added.
- `app-icon.svg`: the app icon, a white g on the black pill square. Home screens and splash only.
- Give the wordmark clear space of at least its x-height on every side. Never recolour, stretch or add effects.
- A bank's own logo is supplied by the bank and sits in its app chrome, not inside Gratifi's cards.
