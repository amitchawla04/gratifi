UNDER THE HOOD

Every answer passes five steps before a member sees it.

1

Know

Reads the member's live balance, tier, orders, offers and history, plus your rules and catalogue.

2

Decide

Picks the best answer or next step for this member, inside the limits you set.

3

Check

A separate check tests local rules, spend limits, fraud signals and tone. Every number is checked against the points ledger.

4

Act

Answers, or redeems, books or pays out once the member says yes. Every action is logged.

5

Learn

What the member did next makes the next suggestion better.

It runs on a leading AI model, swapped when a better one arrives. The checks don't change.
