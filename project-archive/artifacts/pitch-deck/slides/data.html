APPENDIX · DATA AND CONTROLS

Your members’ data stays yours.

AI disclosure

Each conversation opens by saying the assistant is an AI, in fixed wording (EU AI Act, Article 50).

Consent

Members opt in before any message and can delete what the assistant remembers (UK and EU GDPR).

Separate data

Each brand’s data is kept separate, as is each member’s assistant.

Spend limits

You set the limits. Anything above them needs your approval.

Full action log

Every action is recorded, and most can be reversed from your control room.

Independent testing

An outside security test runs before launch.

No model training

Your members’ data is never used to train AI models.

Your processor

We act as your data processor under a standard agreement.

We’ll take your security team through the detail.
