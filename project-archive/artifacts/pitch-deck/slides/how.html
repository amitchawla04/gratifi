APPENDIX · HOW IT WORKS

Every answer goes through five steps.

1. Know

Reads the member’s live balance, tier, offers and history, alongside your rules and rewards.

2. Decide

Picks the best next step for that member, within the limits you set.

3. Check

A separate check verifies every number and applies market rules and spend limits.

4. Act

Answers, or redeems and books once the member says yes.

5. Learn

What the member does next shapes the next suggestion, within the limits you set.

From your systems

Balances, expiry dates, tiers, eligibility, prices, redemptions and terms.

From the AI

Understanding the member, wording, timing and which eligible offer to suggest.

Runs on a leading AI model, named in your contract. We upgrade as better ones arrive.
