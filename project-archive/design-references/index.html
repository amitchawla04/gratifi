What makes the look

Calm ground, white objects
  • Warm grey canvas, or a near-white screen
  • Pure white cards with big corners (24 to 32px)
  • Soft, wide shadows. No borders, no gradients
One hot accent
  • A single orange for selection, progress and done
  • Everything else is black, white and grey
  • Paper colours (yellow, pale blue) only on notes
Things you can hold
  • Polaroids at a slight tilt, paper clips, stamps with perforated edges, die-cut stickers
  • Photos peeking out of folders
  • Handwriting on notes, nowhere else
Big, confident type
  • Bold titles with tight spacing
  • Grey meta lines split by dots
  • Small letter-spaced labels (like VOLUME)
Controls people already know
  • Round white icon buttons, one black primary
  • Black pill for the selected tab or row
  • Floating nav bar, calendar, avatars with checks
Motion that confirms
  • The check pops, the range stretches, the dial turns
  • Movement only when something happened

The rule for agentic screens

  • The assistant suggests. The member decides with a normal button.
  • Every action the assistant takes lands in a familiar place: basket, calendar, receipt, wallet.
  • There is always a manual way to do the same thing: browse, search, pick.
  • Chat is available, but it is not the home screen.
  • Numbers on screen come from the brand's systems, never from the AI.

How we write

  • Write the way people talk. Short words, short sentences.
  • Lead with what the reader cares about. Reasons second.
  • Numbers instead of adjectives. No number we can't source.
  • Active voice: always clear who does what.
  • UK spelling for UK and EU work.
  • Never: seamless, unlock, leverage, empower, elevate, transform, revolutionise, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, ecosystem, journey, delve, robust, "it's not X, it's Y", rhetorical openers, lists of three adjectives.
