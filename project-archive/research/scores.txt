## How the score works
Each company gets up to 100 points across five parts. Every point has to come from a fact with a source; no source, no points.
|ROW ||

|ROW || 
Money || 
Do they spend a lot on rewards? || 
25 = published spend or a huge member base; 15 = big program, no spend figure; 5 = small or unknown
|ROW || 
Timing || 
Did they do something public in the last 12 months? (launch, relaunch, new partners, complaints, boss naming it a priority) || 
25 = in the last 3 months; 15 = in the last 12 months; 5 = nothing found
|ROW || 
Fit || 
Do they need what only we bring together: rewards, delivery, agents, fraud guard? || 
20 = need all of it; 10 = need some; 5 = software only
|ROW || 
Open door || 
Can an outsider get in? || 
15 = no vendor, or unhappy with one; 8 = uses vendors, could switch; 3 = locked in with a rival, builds in-house, or buys by public tender
|ROW || 
Our markets || 
Is it decided in India, UAE or Singapore? || 
15 = decided in India, UAE or Singapore; 8 = rest of the Gulf or South-East Asia, or a head office abroad decides; 3 = outside these regions
What the score means: 75 or more = call this week. 60 to 74 = worth a pitch. Under 60 = watch for now.
What it can't tell you: whether we know the person who decides. That usually matters most, and only your team knows it. Add up to 10 points for a warm introduction.
