.5853 h=a718e5a7 This is a white-label customer engagement platform. An enterprise puts its own brand on it, plugs it into its apps, and it's live for its customers straight away. When it wants more to offer members, it switches on our content, built on 17 years of buying, delivering and paying out rewards across India, the Gulf and Singapore.

.43235 h=b58c00ec ## Terms used in this doc
LIST .56388 h=5ce5aaa3
   - .56389: Platform: The white-label product itself. It carries the brand's name, lives inside the brand's apps, and runs on the brand's products, rewards, rules and data.
   - .56551: Content add-on: R360's catalogue, travel, experiences, delivery and cash payouts. A brand switches it on inside the platform when it wants more to offer.
   - .56706: Playbook: A ready-made program for one audience, say electricians, with the rules, screens and messaging already built.
   - .56827: Agents: There are six. The setup agent designs a program and launches it. The run agent manages offers day to day and wins back members who've gone quiet.    - .57248: Control room: Where the brand's loyalty and finance teams, and our program team, approve what the agents propose, set spend limits, track results and roll back actions.
   - .57418: Active member: Anyone who earned or spent points at least once in the calendar month. Opening a message doesn't count.

.14313 h=9b09e84f ## How a program goes live
.14337 h=08e28b83 For a full program, we're aiming to go live in about 7 days. Today it takes 3 to 18 months. Seven days is still a target, not a fact. The first three pilots have to prove it.
LIST .37570 h=ccb5319f
   - .37571: Check the data (before signing). Our agents spend a day looking at a sample of the brand's data. If it's too messy, we tell them straight away and quote for cleaning it up.
   - .37745: Connect (day 1). The brand gives us access to its customer list, sales data, and app or WhatsApp number. For shopkeepers and tradespeople we also connect the distributor system or the pack codes. If there's an old supplier, the switch agent moves members and points across.
   - .38020: Set the goal and pick a playbook (day 1). The brand tells us what it wants, for example "get electricians to scan 20% more packs this quarter." The setup agent drafts the rules, the rewards and what it's likely to cost.
   - .38241: Choose the rewards (day 2). The brand's own, ours, cash to bank, or a mix.
   - .38317: Approve (days 2 to 3). The brand reviews the program, the cost estimate, sample screens and messages, and how tax will work. A person signs off the legal terms.
   - .38479: Test with a small group (days 3 to 5). About 1% of members. The guard agent watches for fraud and unexpected costs.
   - .38596: Go live (by day 7). The run and member agents take over, and the brand keeps an eye on things from the control room.
.15515 h=ef67c7c7 Moving an existing program onto the platform follows steps 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7, and takes 2 to 3 weeks. The content and payout add-ons can be switched on at step 4 for any program.
