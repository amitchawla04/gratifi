Sitemap
Everything a customer can do by talking to Gratifi in the Barclaycard app, in twelve areas. Each area breaks into sections, then journeys, then the detail of each journey: what customers say, what Gratifi does, what it shows, the actions it offers, and the rules it follows. Items marked Business apply to business cards only.
1 Front door and settings
1.1 Ways in
Ask bar on every screen
Sits above the tab bar on tab screens and at the foot of every other screen
Placeholder changes by screen: "Ask about this payment" on a payment, "Ask about your statement" on a statement
Opens the conversation with the screen's context already attached, so "can I split this?" knows which payment
Microphone
Press to talk, release to send; live words shown as the customer speaks
Falls back to typing with a short note if speech isn't available
Note on Home
One note at a time, from Proactive notes (area 10)
Tapping the button acts; tapping the note opens a conversation about it
"Ask about this"
On every payment, statement, benefit, offer and booking
Opens with a suggested question, for example "Tell me about the Volt Electricals payment"
Notifications
A push note opens straight into a conversation with the answer already there
In-app search
Typing in search answers the question at the top, with matching screens underneath
Deep links from the bank
Emails and texts from the bank can open a Gratifi conversation on a set topic, never with a login link in the message

2.2 What's due
When's my payment due?
Shows: payment card with minimum, statement balance, due date, Direct Debit status
Charge card: the full statement amount, never a minimum

Why is my minimum this amount?
Breaks the minimum into its parts: 1%, interest, fees, plan instalments
Will I pay interest?
Yes or no from the rules engine, with the amount that avoids it: "Pay £3,812.40 by Mon 12 Oct and there's no interest on purchases"
Promotional balances

2.3 Make a payment
Pay now

Rules: can't pay more than the balance; charge card offers the statement balance and full balance only

Payment didn't go through
Reads the status and explains; offers to try again or change account
2.4 Direct Debit
Set up or change
Options: statement balance, minimum, fixed amount (charge card: statement balance only)
Rules: a fixed amount under the minimum takes the minimum instead; changes take effect from the next statement if close to the due date

Confirms, reminds of the due date, offers undo
2.5 Payment date

4.4 Fraud and scams

Checks against the bank's rules: never asks for PIN, login details or full passcode; emails never have a login link
If the customer shared details: freeze, then connect to the fraud team straight away
