# Where the money is

The big reward money is not in store shopper programs. It's where brands reward people for selling, recommending or sticking with them: dealers and mechanics, shoppers who scan a pack, policyholders, sales staff. India's dealer and channel loyalty alone is put at ₹26,800 Cr a year ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/indias-rs-26800-crore-channel-loyalty-market-sees-engagement-fatigue-report-91804.htm), a vendor's estimate). So "brands spend less than banks" is true for shopper programs, not for these.

On Pampers: I couldn't find any public budget figure for its program or any other brand's. What is public: Pampers Club is run by an outside vendor, Aimia, and P&G pays for the rewards itself (sources below).

## Go after these three first

Same product, new pitch: "reward the people who sell for you," not "launch a shopper program."

1. **Dealer and mechanic loyalty in India.** The biggest pot, run by small vendors, and its problems match what we built. 51% of partners sit in 6 or more programs and tune out; 64% of brands say physical rewards cost too much to deliver; payouts to bank accounts (UPI) are up 60% ([Almonds report](https://almonds.ai/channel-loyalty-report-2026/)).
   - Call first: Polycab, Castrol, Birla Opus, Coca-Cola India, Hero.
   - Watch out: a 10% tax is cut from partner rewards above ₹20,000 a year, and 58% of partners don't understand it. Fix: our agents explain it on WhatsApp in the partner's language and handle the paperwork. Owner: product lead.
   - Watch out: fake scans and dealers working together to claim rewards. Fix: guard agent with per-code and per-phone limits. Owner: head of risk.
2. **Brand scan-to-earn.** Big consumer-goods brands already pay for this abroad, but none of India's big ones runs one for shoppers. Vendor deals abroad run [$0.5–3M over 2–3 years](https://www.snipp.com/company/news/snipp-interactive-reports-financial-results-for-q4-and-fiscal-2025), before any reward spend.
   - Call first: P&G India, Nestlé India, Mondelez India, Abbott, Kimberly-Clark.
   - Watch out: global brands often pick vendors from head office. Fix: pitch India as a test market, then take the same program to the Gulf and South-East Asia. Owner: CEO.
3. **Insurance wellness.** Insurers pay back real money for healthy habits, and they need places for members to spend it.
   - Call first: Aditya Birla Health, then AIA and Ping An in Asia.
   - Watch out: insurance rules limit what can be given to policyholders. Fix: legal check per market before pitching. Owner: head of legal.
