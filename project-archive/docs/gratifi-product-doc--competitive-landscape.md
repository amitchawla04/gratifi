## If a client brings one of them up

| Situation | How we handle it | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The client runs on Capillary | Offer to supply rewards into the Capillary program. For a full program, lead with rewards, delivery and speed | CEO |
| The client is comparing us with Loylty Rewardz or Vernost | Lead with scale, stock across India, the Gulf and Singapore, and the agents on top | Head of growth, International |
| Almonds, BigCity or NReach already runs the program | Supply our rewards and cash payouts alongside them first | Head of sales, India |
| The client asks what stops others copying our agents | Our real advantage is rewards and delivery at bank scale, and that took 17 years to build | CEO |
