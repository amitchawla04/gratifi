# What we checked

No search can promise it found everything. This one is built so you can see exactly what was covered and what wasn't.

1. **A full grid.** 27 industries against 6 types of person a company rewards. Every square was searched and marked, even when nothing turned up.
2. **Proof for every square.** Four researchers each took a group of industries. Each mark had to be backed by a page they opened; no page, no mark.
3. **A second pair of eyes.** A fifth researcher, who hadn't seen the work, looked for what was missed. What it found is listed below.
4. **Open items listed.** Anything not checked is named at the bottom, so nothing is quietly skipped.
