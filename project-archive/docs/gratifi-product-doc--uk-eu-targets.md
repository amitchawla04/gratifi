# UK & EU targets

100 UK and EU companies, scored the same way as the first list: money (25), timing (25), fit (20), open door (15) and our markets (15), out of 100. A warm introduction adds up to 10 more; sales adds those. Companies building their own AI, locked into another provider, or with no appetite are capped at 74, so they don't crowd the call list. Checked in September 2026 against each company's pages, app stores and press.

## What we found

- **2026 is a year of cuts.** Flying Blue, Club Eurostar, TAP, Vueling, Accor+, McDonald's UK, Asda, NatWest, Amex and Barclays all made rewards harder to earn or quicker to lose. Members are confused and angry, and an assistant that tells them the best move right now is the easiest pitch.
- **Germany is leaving shared programs.** REWE and Decathlon left PAYBACK, Lidl moved to points, and DeutschlandCard shuts on 30 November 2026, leaving partners like Esso with no program.
- **Almost no one has an assistant that acts for the member.** PAYBACK, Zalando, IHG, Revolut, Lloyds, bunq and Trainline are building their own AI, but none of it redeems, books or cashes out for the member. Pitch Gratifi to them as the rewards expert that plugs into their assistant.
- **Trade and telecom are wide open.** Toolstation, Screwfix, Wickes, Travis Perkins, British Gas, Vodafone, EE and Deutsche Telekom run flat discounts or prize draws with no personal nudges, and no rival vendor is visible.
- **easyJet is the clearest single target.** It is building a loyalty program from scratch for early 2027 and hasn't named a tech partner.
