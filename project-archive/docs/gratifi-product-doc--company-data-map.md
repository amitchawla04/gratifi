# Company data map

For each of the 99 companies in Deal scores: what they run today, what data they could plug in, where the gap is, which modules we'd switch on, and one thing their customer's assistant could say on day one. Checked in September 2026 against company pages, app-store listings and press. The assistant lines are illustrative, written by us; the brand names stay the brand's.

## What the 99 have in common

| What we found | Companies | What it means for the build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No standing program at all | 14 | They need the full stack: points, rules, rewards and an assistant. Setup agent and playbooks carry most of the work. |
| Points buy little: only the brand's own products or flights, a till discount, or a thin catalogue | 16 | The content add-on is the easiest yes: catalogue, travel and experiences on top of their points. |
| Cashback, discounts or a paid plan, nothing to earn toward | 11 | Points, tiers and challenges on top of what they already pay for. |
| Members report unpaid rewards, failed KYC, broken logins or no support | 23 | Mostly trade apps. Guard agent, tracked cash payouts and a WhatsApp assistant that answers "where's my money" fix the pain they already have. |
| Already runs its own AI assistant for members | 1 | Al-Futtaim Blue. Content add-on only. |
| Working program; what's missing is personal help and more to offer | 38 | The assistant is the sell: it knows the member and acts for them. |

4 companies fall in two rows (no program and broken operations), so the rows add to more than 99.

**What they'd switch on.** Of the 99, 96 need an assistant, in the app, on WhatsApp or both. Then, in order: rewards catalogue 50, experiences 36, points and tiers 33, personal offers 23, challenges, targets and streaks 22, cash payouts 18, travel 16, privileges 15, scan and claim 8, wellness rewards 5. So Phase 1 builds the assistant, the guard agent and the points engine, with the content add-on live from day one; everything else is a module a brand adds when it wants it.

## The module catalogue

A brand builds its program the way someone sets up a phone: connect what they have, switch on what they want, choose where their customers meet it. Every module has a ready-made connector or a file upload, the guard agent checks it against that market's rules before it goes live, and the brand can switch it off from the control room at any time.

### 3. Choose where members meet it

| Surface | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| In-app assistant | Lives inside the brand's own app, in the brand's name |
| WhatsApp assistant | Same assistant on WhatsApp, in local languages; the only surface most shopkeepers and tradespeople use |
| Web | A branded page for members without the app |
| AI shopping-assistant feed | Offers and rewards published so outside AI assistants can find them |

### 4. Add R360 content (optional)

| Add-on | What it brings |
| --- | --- |
| Rewards catalogue | Products and vouchers to spend points on |
| Travel | Flights and hotels |
| Experiences | Dining, events, spa, activities |
| Cash payouts | Points straight to the member's bank, tracked |
| Delivery | Fulfilment and tracking |

### Always on, for every brand

The six agents (setup, run, member, guard, insight, switch), the control room, a full audit trail of everything the assistant did, and member memory with a "forget" control. The guard agent approves anything that spends money or touches a member's account before it happens.
